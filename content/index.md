Welcome aboard the **Endurance**.

In *Interstellar*, the Endurance is a ring of modules spinning in the dark, carrying a small crew through a wormhole to look for a new home. This blog borrows that idea. It is a ship for long trips into hard subjects, logging what turns up along the way.

## The crew

* **Cooper**: the pilot. He takes the controls and asks the questions, which is the role of the writer here.
* **TARS**: honest, dependable, set to the right level of humour. TARS stands for the practical side of this blog: notes, commands and checklists that work when you need them.
* **CASE**: quiet and steady, holding the ship together while everyone else makes noise. CASE stands for the patient work that gets done in the background.
* **Dr. Brand**: the scientist, daughter of Professor Brand. She reads the research, weighs the theory and insists the work holds up. Dr. Brand stands for the careful, evidence-led side of this blog: the "why" behind each note.

## Mission rules

1. **Expect everything.** "Murphy's law doesn't mean that something bad will happen. It means that whatever can happen, will happen."
2. **Keep going when it gets hard.** Some docking manoeuvres look impossible, and some topics too. As Cooper tells TARS: *"It's not possible." / "No. It's necessary."*
3. **Remember why we fly.** "Love is the one thing we're capable of perceiving that transcends dimensions of time and space." (Brand)
